Questions

What members ask first.

Anything further is covered on your consultation call.

What is the cost of membership?⌄

Membership is tailored to the level of service you require. Your consultant presents the available options during your initial consultation, with no obligation to proceed.

How is my privacy protected?⌄

Your details are held in strict confidence and are never displayed publicly. You choose what is shared, and only verified profiles matched to your criteria are introduced to you.

Who will I be introduced to?⌄

A dedicated matchmaker curates a small shortlist of verified members whose background, values, and expectations align with yours — rather than an open database you search yourself.

How does this differ from a matrimony website?⌄

A matrimony site gives you a search bar. VIP Shaadi gives you a consultant who does the searching, verifies each profile, and arranges introductions on your behalf — a managed service, not a self-serve listing.
